Version: 1.0

# Project Name

Inventory Management System

---

# Business Overview

The Inventory Management System is designed to help wholesale and retail businesses efficiently manage their inventory, suppliers, customers, purchases, sales, payments, and reports.

The system replaces manual record keeping with a centralized, secure, and scalable digital platform.

---

# Business Problem

The current manual inventory process results in:

- Data duplication
- Stock inaccuracies
- Human errors
- Slow invoice generation
- Difficult report preparation
- Poor inventory visibility
- Limited accountability

---

# Proposed Solution

Develop a web-based Inventory Management System using Laravel 13 that provides:

- Secure authentication
- Role-based access control
- Inventory tracking
- Purchase management
- Sales management
- Customer payment tracking
- Reporting dashboard
- Audit logging

---

# Business Goals

- Improve inventory accuracy
- Reduce manual work
- Increase operational efficiency
- Provide real-time stock visibility
- Generate business reports instantly
- Improve data security
- Enable future business expansion

---

# Expected Benefits

Business Benefits

- Faster business operations
- Better stock control
- Improved decision making
- Reduced operational cost

Technical Benefits

- Scalable architecture
- Secure application
- Maintainable codebase
- AI-assisted development workflow

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- All business modules are operational
- Inventory updates in real time
- Reports are generated accurately
- Security standards are maintained
- Users can perform daily operations without manual records
